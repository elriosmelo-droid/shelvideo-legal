Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2025

ShelVideo ("the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

1. Data We Collect

The App collects only the minimum data required to operate:

  • TikTok OAuth access token and refresh token (stored locally on your device)
  • TikTok user ID and display name (to identify the authorized account)
  • Video metadata (title, upload status) stored in a local database

2. How We Use Your Data

All collected data is used exclusively to authenticate your TikTok account and post content on your behalf. We do not sell, share, or transmit your data to any third party.

3. Data Storage

All data is stored locally on your own device. No data is stored on external servers controlled by ShelVideo.

4. TikTok API

This App uses the TikTok Content Posting API. Your use of TikTok features is also governed by TikTok's Privacy Policy at tiktok.com/legal/privacy-policy.

5. Data Deletion

You can revoke the App's access to your TikTok account at any time via TikTok Settings → Security → Authorized Apps. Deleting the App's local files removes all stored data.

6. Children's Privacy

This App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

7. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy at any time. Continued use of the App constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, contact us via TikTok: @shelvideo
